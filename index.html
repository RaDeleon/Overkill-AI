Assumptions behind the numbers
  • Anthropic doesn't publish how big a session or a week is. A Pro 5-hour session is taken as about 45 short messages on a mid-size model; a Pro week as about 15 Pro sessions. Max is 5× Pro for both; Ultra is 10× Pro per week with no 5-hour limit once verified.
  • Models are weighted by their relative compute cost (published API prices), since heavier models use limits faster.
  • Thinking grows with effort; agent and coding work counts several turns, with repeat context at cached weight.
  • Every percentage here is an estimate. The comparisons between models are more reliable than the exact numbers.
